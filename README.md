# 🌐“Multifunctional Smart Window System with Dual Energy Harvesting, Air Purification, Rainwater Harvesting, and IoT Monitoring

## 🔹 Overview
CLEAR Window is a **next‑generation multifunctional smart window** that integrates:
- Transparent solar PV + thermoelectric modules for **24/7 energy generation**
- **Electrostatic + photocatalytic air purification** (maintenance‑free)
- **Rainwater harvesting channels** for clean water collection
- **Electrochromic tint control** for comfort and efficiency
- **IoT monitoring** for real‑time data on power, AQI, humidity, and water volume

---

## ⚡ Features
- **Dual Energy Harvesting** → Solar + Thermoelectric  
- **Air Purification** → Transparent electrostatic plates + TiO₂ coating  
- **Rainwater Harvesting** → Built‑in micro‑channels with auto‑cleaning  
- **Smart Tint Control** → AI‑driven electrochromic layer  
- **Energy Storage** → Thin supercapacitors embedded in frame  
- **IoT Monitoring** → Sensors for AQI, humidity, water, and energy output  

---

## 🚀 Advantages
- Generates electricity **day & night**  
- **No consumable filters** → 15–20 years lifespan  
- Provides **clean air + clean water** alongside energy  
- ROI: ~5–6 years payback in urban India  
- Scalable for **smart cities, offices, and eco‑homes**

---

## 🛠 Components
- Transparent PV film (perovskite/organic hybrid)  
- Thermoelectric modules (Bi₂Te₃)  
- Electrostatic plates (ITO/graphene coated)  
- TiO₂ photocatalytic mesh  
- Rainwater harvesting channels (stainless steel/PVC)  
- Electrochromic film  
- IoT sensors (AQI, humidity, water, power)  
- Supercapacitor storage  

---

## 📌 Installation (Prototype)
1. Assemble tempered glass frame with PV + thermoelectric layers.  
2. Integrate electrostatic plates + photocatalytic mesh.  
3. Add rainwater channels and connect to storage tank.  
4. Apply electrochromic film for tint control.  
5. Embed IoT sensors and connect to ESP32/Arduino board.  
6. Link supercapacitor storage for energy buffering.  

---

## 🧩 Usage
- Install on building façade or residential windows.  
- Connect IoT dashboard for real‑time monitoring.  
- Rainwater harvesting auto‑flush keeps system clean.  
- Continuous air purification + energy generation.
